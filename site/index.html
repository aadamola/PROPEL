Questions Lagos always asks.

Will an AI assistant annoy my buyers?

It answers only from the facts sheet you sign. It never guesses prices or title status, never negotiates, and hands anything sensitive straight to your team. What actually annoys buyers is silence.

Can you guarantee sales?

No one honest can. We guarantee the system: every enquiry answered, every hot buyer handed over, every lead on the record. On Launch Partner we're paid partly on sales the ledger proves we brought you, so our incentive is the same as yours.

How fast can it go live?

We confirm the date after a one-hour check of your Meta access, because Meta's own verification sets part of the timeline and we don't promise what we don't control.

Do you verify the properties you market?

We vet every client and work only from facts warranted in writing. We don't market land with a disputed title, at any fee.

Will it use my sales team's WhatsApp number?

Never. The core runs on Instagram. If you add WhatsApp, it goes on a dedicated new number, so nobody's working phone is touched.

Do you handle my ad budget?

Never. Ad spend goes from you directly to Meta. We manage the campaigns; your money stays yours.
