# Demo kit: the "assistant on their phone" pattern

*What's left of the July pitch pack, kept because it's the pattern for every Response Audit demo. The v1 product demos (Presence, Leads, Launch, Studio) were retired with rate card v1.*

| File | What | Reuse for a new prospect |
|---|---|---|
| `concierge-demo.html` | Interactive tap-through of a buyer chatting with the assistant, on any phone | Copy it, swap in the prospect's **public** listing facts, label it DEMO |
| `05-concierge-brief.md` | Send-ready brief explaining the DM + comment system | Adapt the names; keep the "only says what you've signed" framing |

## Rules (unchanged)
1. **Pitch-only.** A demo is never published or presented as client work.
2. **Public facts only**, every placeholder marked. On signing, the warranted facts sheet replaces everything.
3. **The demo goes first** in the audit (60 seconds, on their phone); the offer comes second. This is the one sales sequence we've seen close.
