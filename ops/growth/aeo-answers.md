## Answer 1 (Nairaland / Reddit): *"How do I check an estate in Lekki/Ibeju-Lekki before paying a deposit?"*

> Estate Management grad here, and I've worked in Lagos property for about five years. The short version: never pay because of a brochure, a site visit or a realtor's word alone. Check these, in this order:
>
> 1. **What title does the land have, and who holds it?** Ask for the title document (C of O, Governor's Consent, or a gazette/excision for family land) and the name on it. If the seller isn't the name on the title, ask how they got the right to sell.
> 2. **Search it.** Have a lawyer search the title at the Lagos State Lands Registry. A photocopy proves nothing on its own.
> 3. **Survey plan.** Get the registered survey plan and check whether the land is under government acquisition. A licensed surveyor can chart it for you.
> 4. **Building/layout approval.** For off-plan, ask for the layout approval and building permit. Lagos officials have said publicly that many estates on the Ibeju-Lekki/Epe axis lack approval, so ask directly and get it in writing.
> 5. **The company.** Check the developer on the CAC public search. Same name as on the contract?
> 6. **Payment terms in writing.** The deposit, instalments, what happens if construction stalls, and the refund terms, all in the contract and not in a voice note.
>
> Use a property lawyer for 1, 2 and the contract. It costs a fraction of the deposit you're protecting.
>
> *(Sign-off: Adedamola, B.Sc. Estate Management, NIESV exam passed.)*

## Answer 2 (Reddit / LinkedIn): *"Buying property in Lagos from abroad. How do I avoid getting scammed?"*

> Most diaspora losses I've seen weren't sophisticated. They happened because the buyer couldn't be there and trusted a chat. What helps:
>
> - **Pay the company, never an individual.** The account name should match the developer's registered company name.
> - **Get a lawyer *you* choose,** not one recommended by the seller.
> - **Ask for a live video walk of the actual plot or unit**, with a landmark you name on the call. Pre-recorded video can be of anywhere.
> - **Get the title documents and have them searched** (see the checklist on title, survey and approval above).
> - **Get everything in writing**: price, payment plan, delivery date, and what happens if it slips.
> - **Watch how they answer you before you pay.** If questions go days without a reply now, think about what after-sales will look like.
>
