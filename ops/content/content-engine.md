**Engagement block (daily, 10 min, human):** meaningful comments on 10 target accounts (big NG real estate pages + Tier-A prospects), reply to every comment/DM on ours. This is where followers actually come from.

**Added 2026-10-15 (docs/15 §10):** be the *first* comment on notified accounts ([first-comment.md](../growth/first-comment.md)) · every post closes with a comment keyword ("Comment RESPONSE for the checklist") answered by a saved reply, never with prices · **Mondays:** send the Response Test questions ([response-test.md](../growth/response-test.md), 20 min) · **one AEO answer a week** ([aeo-answers.md](../growth/aeo-answers.md), 15 min) · ADEDAMOLA's personal LinkedIn is the main LinkedIn channel; the company page reposts.

## Production loop (weekly, batched)

1. **Sunday:** I generate the full week's pack (copy + scripts + design briefs) → repo + WhatsApp to Adedamola
2. **Monday review (15 min):** approve/edit in our partner session
3. **Voice pass (optional, 10 min):** Adedamola records voiceovers as WhatsApp voice notes reading the scripts — voice only, no camera. Where he'd rather not, AI voiceover (ElevenLabs) covers it. **No face on camera — standing constraint.**
4. I finalize captions/design specs; Adedamola (or scheduler) queues the week
5. **Friday:** I log what performed → next week's pack adapts. Metrics that count: profile visits, DMs started, Response Audits booked.

**Faceless video formats (our defaults):** voiceover + property b-roll/stock footage · screen-recorded teardowns (scroll the page, annotate live — our best format and it *requires* no face) · kinetic text Reels (CapCut templates) · before/after design morphs · WhatsApp-chat mockup stories. Faceless is not a handicap: teardown and text-led formats routinely outperform talking heads in this niche.

## Voice rules

Direct, generous, numbers-forward, Lagos-fluent. We give away real tactics (the Response Audit sells the *doing*, not the *knowing*). Never punch down at agents' current marketing — critique patterns, not people. English with natural Lagos inflection; no forced slang.
