## The short version

> You add **Propel as a Partner** in your Meta Business account using our Business ID. **No email, no password, no login of yours ever leaves your company.** You choose exactly which accounts we can touch, and you can remove us in two clicks whenever you like. ## Step 1 — Open your Business settings

1. Go to **business.facebook.com** and log in with your normal Facebook account.
2. Top-left, make sure your **company's business account** is selected (not a personal one).
3. Click **Settings** (the ⚙️ gear, bottom-left).

## Step 2 — Add Propel as a partner

1. In the left menu: **Users → Partners**.
2. Click **Add** → **Give a partner access to your assets**.
3. Paste Propel's Business ID: **`[PROPEL BUSINESS ID]`** → **Next**.

> **No email is needed.** Meta connects the two businesses by ID. If any screen asks for an email address, you're on the "invite people" page instead of "Partners" — go back to **Users → Partners**.

## Step 3 — Choose what Propel can access

Meta now asks which accounts to share. Pick each one below and set the permission shown. **Leave everything else off.**

| Account type | Which one | Permission to give | Why we need it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Instagram accounts** | Your business Instagram | **Full control** (everything) | To connect the assistant to your DMs and manage content |
| **Pages** | Your Facebook Page | **Messages** (+ Content if we post for you) | Instagram messaging runs through the connected Page |
| **WhatsApp accounts** | The assistant's WhatsApp account *(if it already exists)* | **Full control** | To connect the number and run the assistant |
| **Ad accounts** *(only if we run your ads)* | Your ad account | **Manage campaigns** | To build and run campaigns; **your card stays yours and you see every naira spent** |

Click **Save** (or **Assign**). If an account isn't listed in Step 3, it hasn't been added to your Business account yet — tell us and we'll do it with you on a quick call.

## Step 4 — Check it worked

Back on **Users → Partners**, you should see **Propel** in the list. Click it to see exactly what we can access. Send us a quick "done" on WhatsApp and we take it from there.

## What you're agreeing to (and what you're not)

- **You own everything.** The Instagram, Page, WhatsApp number, every conversation and every ad stay in your company's account.
- **We get only what you ticked.** We can't see your other accounts, payment methods or other people's access.
- **You never share a password with us.** If anyone — including us — asks for your password, say no. This method exists so that never has to happen.
- **You can remove us at any time:** **Settings → Users → Partners → Propel → Remove**. It takes effect immediately, no need to ask us.

## If something goes wrong

| What you see | What it means |
|---|---|
| No **Partners** option, or the **Add** button is greyed out | Your login isn't an admin. Ask whoever is admin to do it, or to make you admin first |
| "Business ID not found" | A digit is off — copy-paste the ID instead of typing it |
| The account you want isn't in the list | It isn't in your Business account yet. Add it under **Accounts** first, or we'll do it together |
| It asks for an email | You're on **People**, not **Partners** — see Step 2 |

## Internal notes (delete before sending to a client)

- **On our side after they add us:** the shared assets land in Propel's portfolio but nobody is assigned yet. Go to **Settings → Accounts → [asset] → Assign people** and add ourselves before trying to use them, or the asset looks "missing".
- **Least privilege is the pitch, not just hygiene.** Ask for full control only where the Concierge build needs it (Instagram, WhatsApp). Page = Messages unless we post for them; ad account only if ads are in the SOW.
- **Meta renames these menus regularly** (Business Manager → Business Suite → Business portfolio). Re-check the click-path against the live UI before each new client and update this file if it has moved.
- **Does not replace business verification** (Security Centre → Start verification, client's CAC). That runs in parallel — see `04-meta-access-pack.md` step 3 and `ops/setup/SOP-01-client-waba-onboarding.md`.
- Never ask a client for passwords or 2FA codes. If a client offers them, decline and send this guide.
