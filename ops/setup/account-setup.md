- **No bought followers, engagement pods or fake reviews, ever.** Audit tools expose them in seconds, and we sell accountability.
- **Proof that needs no followers:** the credential · the Response Test data (aggregate) · a named client · real monthly-report numbers once we have them.
- **Reach without followers:** slideshows, comment keywords, first-comment engagement, AEO answers and group owners ([ops/growth/](../growth/README.md)).
- **What we track for ourselves:** profile visits, DMs started and audits booked. Follower count is reported to nobody.
