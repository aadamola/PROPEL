# The Lagos Response Test: protocol and scripts

*Plan and rationale: [docs/15](../../docs/15-positioning-and-growth.md) §5 move 1 + §10. Tracker: [response-test.csv](response-test.csv). ADEDAMOLA sends; I log, follow up and report.*

## The loop (20 min/week for ADEDAMOLA)

1. **Monday:** I put 10 firms at the top of `response-test.csv` with their Instagram handles checked. ⚠️ Every handle starts as `[VERIFY]`. Search the firm on Instagram and confirm it's the real account (verified badge, estate photos, consistent name) before sending.
2. **You send the question** (below) to each, from your own account. Screenshot the sent messages, or just tell me the time you sent them.
3. **Through the week:** when a reply arrives, screenshot it (the timestamp is enough). I fill `first_reply_at` and `reply_hours`.
4. **The next Monday:** I draft each firm's give-first follow-up. You send it.
5. **Anyone who replies to the follow-up** gets the demo offer (below), and I build their demo.

## Script 1: the question (send exactly this; it's a real question)

> Hi, what's the current price and payment plan on [estate]?

That's all. No introduction, no Propel mention. **We genuinely want the answer**: it feeds our corridor pricing for Market Saturday.

**Never:**
- Claim to be a buyer.
- Invent a budget, a name or a timeline.
- Book an inspection.
- Keep a salesperson talking. If they ask *"are you buying?"*, answer truthfully: *"I'm researching current pricing in the corridor, thanks for this."*

## Script 2: the give-first follow-up (one week later, no pitch)

> Hi [name], we're measuring how fast Lagos estates answer Instagram enquiries. Yours: we asked on [day, time], first reply [N hours] later. The median across the [N] estates we've measured so far is [X hours]. That's yours to keep, no pitch attached. If it's useful, I'll send you the full report when it's out.

**If they never replied:**
> Hi [name], we're measuring how fast Lagos estates answer Instagram enquiries. We asked about [estate] on [day, time] and it hasn't been answered yet. It may have been missed in the inbox, which is exactly what we're studying. Happy to share the full report when it's out.

**Rules:**
- Real numbers only.
- Send the median only once at least 10 firms have been measured. Before that, leave the median line out.
- Never compare them to a named competitor.

## Script 3: when they reply (now you may offer, and keep it small)

> Glad it's useful. Quick one: I built a 2-minute demo of what an instant reply looks like, using [estate]'s public listing. Want to see it on your phone? No call needed, I can send a video.

**Before any demo or pitch:** run the vetting checks (rule 5) and the client-conflict check. **`conflict_with_client = YES` means no pitch until ADEDAMOLA has checked the Shalom Park agreement for exclusivity.**

## Script 4: the report (everyone, at about 8 weeks)

> Hi [name], here's the Lagos Property Response Report we mentioned: [link]. All figures are aggregate; no estate is named. Your own result is in the note I sent on [date].

## What goes public and what doesn't

| Public (aggregate only) | Private (only to that firm) |
|---|---|
| Median reply time · % unanswered after 24h · out-of-hours gap · corridor price ranges | That firm's own timestamps and result |

**Never public:** any firm's name, handle, screenshot or individual result.
