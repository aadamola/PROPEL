# Founding pilot: application

*Terms live in [docs/03](../../docs/03-services-and-pricing.md) (rate card v2, "Founding pilots"). This file is the application only. **Applications close 15 December 2026.** That date is real: it isn't extended and the offer isn't re-run.*

## Where it's offered
- Script 3 of the Response Test, **only after a firm has replied**. Never in a first message.
- The Response Audit close.
- Not in public posts until we've decided how many applications we can genuinely handle.

## The message (ADEDAMOLA sends)

> We're taking a small number of founding pilots this quarter, and applications close 15 December. If you'd like to be considered, answer these and I'll come back within two working days:

1. Your name, role and company (as registered with CAC)
2. Which estate or listings would the assistant answer for?
3. Roughly how many Instagram enquiries do you get a month?
4. Who answers your DMs today, and until what time?
5. Do you have the title and approval documents for that estate to hand? *(We only work from documents we've sighted.)*
6. Who in your company can grant Meta (Facebook/Instagram) business admin access?

## How we choose (internal)
- **Vetting first** (rule 5): the CAC search and the documents are sighted before anything is promised.
- Prefer real enquiry volume (question 3) and a named Meta admin (question 6). Without a Meta admin, nothing ships.
- **Client conflict:** anyone selling in the same estate area as an existing client is checked against that client's agreement before acceptance.
- **Never say how many places are left.** "A small number" is true; a count we can't stand behind is not.
