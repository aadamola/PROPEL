## What must never appear in a caption

| Never | Because |
|---|---|
| A projected return, yield, or "value will rise" | On the `must_not_say` list the client signed |
| A completion date for the estate | Not in the facts sheet. There isn't one in writing |
| "Only N units left", countdowns, fake deadlines | CLAUDE.md rule 2 |
| A bank account number | Never, anywhere, in any channel |
| A price that is not in the pack | The pack is the signed set |
| Title document reference numbers | Documents not yet sighted by Propel — Gate 2 |

**If a caption needs a fact that isn't in this pack, it doesn't get posted until Collins confirms it in writing.**
