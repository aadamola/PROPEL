# Group owners: the people developers already listen to

*Nevo's single biggest jump (Postiz ~$6K → ~$12K MRR) came from offering community owners something bigger than he asked for: [docs/15 §10](../../docs/15-positioning-and-growth.md). This is our translation. **About ten people, each researched by hand. Never a list scraped at volume** (NDPA, and it contradicts everything we sell).*

## Who counts (in order of value)

| Type | Why they matter | Where to find them (by hand) |
|---|---|---|
| **Realtor-network owners** who sell for developers | Every developer pays them commission and listens to them | Developer launch coverage (e.g. launches that "drew 1,000+ realtors") · realtor award events · IG bios saying "realtor network" |
| **Real estate trainers / coaches** running WhatsApp, Telegram or IG communities | Their students become agents and developers' sales teams | IG / YouTube search: "real estate training Lagos", "realtor academy" |
| **Property lawyers** who do developer due diligence | Developers trust them; the "only warranted facts" pitch is their language | LinkedIn; NBA Lagos property-law events |
| **Nigerian property podcasts / YouTube channels** | Trusted voice to the diaspora buyers developers want | YouTube / Spotify search |
| **NIESV branch figures** | The tribe play (docs/15 move 6) | Branch events, LinkedIn |

**Qualify before contacting:**
- active in the last 30 days;
- an audience of real property people, not bought followers;
- no public fraud or dispute stories (rule 5 applies to whom we associate with too).

## The offer: bring something big, ask for something small

**We give (pick what fits the person):**
1. **A free enquiry assistant for their own business**, e.g. a coach's course enquiries or a realtor network's recruitment DMs. Built by me on our rig, from facts they sign. *This uses our infrastructure, not a client's.*
2. **A named feature in the Lagos Property Response Report** ("with commentary from…").
3. **Introductions** to people in ADEDAMOLA's industry network who'd value them.

**We ask:** one introduction to a developer they work with, **or** a mention when the Response Report comes out. That's all. Never "please promote us".

## Message (ADEDAMOLA sends, from his own account)

> Hi [name], I'm Adedamola, Estate Management grad, NIESV exam passed, and I run Propel. I've followed [specific thing they did, e.g. "your realtor session on off-plan payment plans"]. Two things, no strings: I'm publishing the first data on how fast Lagos estates answer buyer enquiries, and I'd like your take on it for the report. And I build instant-reply assistants for property businesses, so I'd happily set one up free for [their business] if your DMs get busy. Interested in either?

**Rules:**
- Personalise the [specific thing] every time. If you can't name something real they did, don't send it.
- One message plus one follow-up after 7 days, then stop.
- Log every contact in `ops/crm/pipeline.csv` with `source = group_owner`.

## Never
- Pay for a mention without disclosing it as paid (and paying is above the spend gate until a second client).
- Ask his friends to share (founder constraint 2). These are industry contacts approached as business.
- Promise a group owner commission on a sale we haven't made.
